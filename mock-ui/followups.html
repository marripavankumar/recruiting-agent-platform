When a job is filled or closed

Setting a job to Filled or Closed cancels every draft still waiting for approval for that job (outreach, details requests, submissions, follow-ups) and stops its follow-ups. Nothing more is sent (scenario S22).

Pipeline stages recorded on each submission drive the reminders: Submitted → Interview requested → Interview scheduled → Interviewed → Offer extended → Offer accepted → Started, or Client rejected / Offer declined / Candidate withdrawn (with a reason; a withdrawal suggests the next-ranked candidates).
